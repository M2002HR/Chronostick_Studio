# Long-form workflow and gates

The `chronostick-longform-pipeline` skill routes these gates. Review status is recorded in `pipeline-state.json`; a file's existence is not approval. Keep source, text, prompt, image, render, and review artifacts separate.

| Stage | Inputs and work | Required handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00 Intake and example review | User title, historical source if supplied, reference video/URL, matching transcript and selected frames, language and duration target. Preserve supplied files byte-for-byte. Probe and inspect the actual video across its timeline **beside the transcript**; record timecoded visual/editing mechanisms, mismatches, historical risk and unknowns. If the reference transcript is the only story input, mark it as such and let Stage 01 verify claims independently. Follow [`reference-video-workflow.md`](reference-video-workflow.md). | `source/intake.json`, episode manifest, original source when supplied; `source/reference-video/manifest.json` and `source/reference-video/intake-review.md` when video and transcript exist. |
| 01 Research | Extract every claim, date, quotation, cause, number, character, animal, place, map state and disputed anecdote. Resolve transcription errors. Verify against cited reliable sources, record disagreements and unknowns. | `source/claims.json`, `source/research-notes.md`; no unqualified unsupported claim promoted. |
| 02 Spanish story | Build chapter architecture and factual source-to-script trace; write natural Spanish that preserves supplied content. Hook, early title/thumbnail proof, earned reason to continue, chapter questions, payoff and restrained CTA. Use suspense, surprise and situational wit where truthful; vary intensity so serious consequences land. Read aloud and audit every retained/omitted/additional claim. | `script/story-architecture.md`, `script/narration-es.md`, `script/fidelity-ledger.json`; user review of script. |
| 02a Google Vids voice direction (optional) | After script review and before voice generation, follow [`../google-vids-voice-direction.md`](../google-vids-voice-direction.md) when Google Vids is the provider. Keep the clean narration authoritative; prepare and review a separately tagged script. Record used/skipped in the episode manifest. | If used: `script/voiceover-google-vids-es.md` and voice-direction notes. Acceptance still depends on listening to the actual voice in Stage 03. |
| 03 Voice and timing | User supplies accepted voice and word timestamps. If Stage 02a was used, verify the tags were not spoken and the recorded delivery matches the reviewed words and chapter pace. Archive both originals without edits, validate token coverage, monotonic timing, narration end and silence; derive chapter/semantic cue and five-second slot maps. | `audio/` or truthful external record, `timestamps/word-timestamps-source.csv`, `timestamps/timing-map.json`. |
| 04 Visual direction | Analyze supplied reference-video transcript and frames by timecode and evidence type; turn useful techniques into original ChronoStick direction. Set a dynamic chapter rhythm, emotional range, visual humor and map/graphic system. Inventory recurring identities, animals, vehicles and sensitive depictions. | `plan/visual-direction.md`, `plan/reference-video-analysis.md` when example material exists, and initial identity/map/text ledgers. |
| 05 Detailed direction | Map each spoken cue to a five-second slot and its actual timed shots. Carry selected reference-analysis IDs into sequences/shots; specify how the adapted idea changes framing, cut rhythm, action, visual joke, graphic or transition. Resolve chapter transitions and story-state continuity; give each cut a purpose. Mark every exact text exception, map requirement and reference coverage need. | `plan/shot-plan.md`, `plan/story-state-ledger.json`, `plan/reference-manifest.json`, `plan/text-events.json`, `plan/map-manifest.json`. |
| 06 Reference production | Reuse compatible locked assets. Research real identities; generate and review stick identity, world, animal, prop, map, and single-scene anchors in 16:9. Record exact source, prompt, path, hash and supported shots. Count actual shot-plan reuse and add distinct close, medium, wide or alternate physical viewpoints where the voice-led film would feel repetitive, preserving shared identity and geography. | Every planned shot supported by reviewed anchors; unresolved coverage and unreviewed visual fatigue remain gates. |
| 07 Animatic and risk pilot | Make a complete voice-led animatic and judge clarity, length, excitement, humor placement, promise/payoff, repeated anchors and fatigue against the desired reference-guided feel. Test a representative 16:9 final-setting H3 sample, including character, map, in-engine text, physics and audio. Reject a pilot that adds unplanned subjects or alters map geography even when technically successful; revise the affected prompt family before bulk generation. | `plan/animatic-review.md` and `plan/pilot-review.md` with measured findings, settings, resource cost and revisions. |
| 08 Video prompts | Write one pure generator-facing prompt per H3 clip from approved plan/refs and the adapted visual direction. Include ordered Picture mapping, local timing, inventories, state, camera, light, physics, visual timing/reaction where relevant, SFX, resolved end and exact no-music sentence. | Complete pure prompt set; source and state checks pass. |
| 09 Jobs and preflight | Produce deterministic immutable job JSON, unique fixed seeds and revisions, ordered refs, SFX-only settings and batch defaults. Validate all jobs, paths, collisions, capability and chapter grouping. | Repository validator and live-service dry-run pass. This does not launch generation. |
| 10 Generation and review | Launch only with user authorization. Review every actual clip and sound, inspect frames around cuts and all frames with text/maps. Record approval or precise replacement; regenerate only failed clips. | Exactly one approved editorial render per slot and selection provenance. |
| 11 Picture and sound finish | Concatenate selected clips in order; verify duration, sound gaps, continuity, chapter seams, picture quality. Upscale to 1920×1080; use [`../postproduction.md`](../postproduction.md) to mix accepted Spanish voice, choose native-audio mute/preserve, add Arial Bold active-word captions and explicit supplied music/ambience/SFX, QC full playback. | Immutable picture/SFX master and separate Spanish distribution master, probe and review records. |
| 12 YouTube and closeout | Original 16:9 thumbnail, accurate title/description, source links, chapters and end-screen plan. Review platform settings, historical portrayal, language, final audio and actual distribution file. Localizations branch only from approved picture/SFX master. | Package and truthful status; upload/publish only when authorized. |

If a stage has no required input, record `missing` with the next action. If a revision changes a factual assertion, voice timing, identity or map state, trace the impact downstream; do not quietly keep stale prompts or jobs. Once picture is locked, language adaptation follows `docs/localization-workflow.md` without regenerating scenes.

If the user explicitly directs an episode to skip independent historical verification and use a supplied reference account as an assumed premise, record the exception in `source/research-waiver.json` and mark Stage 01 `skipped`. Preserve any earlier research unchanged. Stage 02 may proceed as a source-assumption draft with a complete fidelity ledger; it must not claim the reference account was verified. This exception applies only to the named episode and scope. Review and approval of the script, voice, identities, maps and later production artifacts remain separate decisions.
