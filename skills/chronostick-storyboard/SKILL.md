# ChronoStick Storyboard

Read the episode scenario, Spanish draft, actual source analysis and `docs/reference-first-shorts.md`. Preserve stable shot IDs from sketches through reference coverage and final timing.

## Draw the plan

Produce actual viewable monochrome sketch images under `plan/storyboard/`, with immutable image revisions and an accompanying structured shot list. Use image generation for drawn bitmap boards. A board may contain panels because it is an internal planning artifact; production reference images remain single-scene. Store paste-ready sketch prompts separately from scenario/review notes. Before submitting each generation, archive its exact request text and hash in a provenance snapshot outside the prompt directory; Git-versioned editable prompts alone do not preserve uncommitted rejected attempts.

Use very simple rough human silhouettes, spare contour lines and recognizable primary props. Avoid ChronoStick anatomy/style, polished rendering, facial/costume design, palette or a detailed period likeness. Show only composition, exact visible counts, one action, camera/framing, main prop and the meaningful change across a cut. Do not use stick-person glyphs as a substitute for the requested neutral human sketches. Preserve a mask's necessary silhouette as a story prop without locking final production art.

Cover every intended shot, including hook, reactions/inserts, discoveries, transitions and payoff. Map panels to source-analysis IDs and scenario beats. Notes may sit outside the sketches; readable planning IDs are not permission for generated screen text. Label all pre-voice times as provisional and keep the total within the chosen 20–30-second Shorts target.

## Review

Inspect every generated panel against its written shot: inventory, composition, action, causal clarity, duplicate/extra subjects, ordering, generation feasibility and coverage. A beautiful board with missing or wrong panels is incomplete. Correct mismatches or explicitly revise the scenario, preserve failed attempts, and prepare the complete package before asking for user review. Do not fabricate approval.

Scenario and storyboard require the user's real review before production reference generation. Pass the reviewed panel inventory to `chronostick-references`: single-person character identities and enough single-scene anchors for the required camera views, props and states. Neutral sketches never become H3 style/identity anchors. Significant revisions after voice timing reopen the affected scenario/sketch/reference decisions, not the entire episode by default.
