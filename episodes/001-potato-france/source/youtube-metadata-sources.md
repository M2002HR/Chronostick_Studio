# YouTube metadata research sources

Checked on 2026-09-27 for the Episode 001 YouTube package.

## Historical sources

- [French Ministry of Agriculture — Antoine Parmentier, la science au service des Hommes](https://agriculture.gouv.fr/antoine-parmentier-la-science-au-service-des-hommes): documents Parmentier's advocacy for potatoes, his Sablons field, and explicitly presents the armed daytime guard and nighttime theft episode as legend (`La légende raconte`).
- [Château de Versailles — Antoine Augustin Parmentier](https://en.chateauversailles.fr/discover/history/great-characters/antoine-augustin-parmentier): documents Parmentier's agricultural work, the Sablons plantings outside Paris, and his 1789 treatise on cultivating and using potatoes.

## YouTube platform sources

- [Upload YouTube videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407?hl=en): 100-character title limit, 5,000-character description limit, required audience decision, age restriction, language, location, category, and related-video fields.
- [Add tags to your YouTube videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en): tags mainly help with misspellings and otherwise play a minimal discovery role.
- [Add custom thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en): recommends a 9:16 Shorts thumbnail at 2160×3840 and notes that custom Shorts thumbnails are added in YouTube Studio on a computer.
- [Find playlists and videos using hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en-15): hashtags must be relevant; excessive or misleading hashtags can be ignored or penalized.
- [Determining if content is made for kids](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en): the creator must make the audience decision; family-friendly or animated content is not automatically made for kids.
- [Age-restrict your own video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2950063?hl=en): the creator must decide whether material is inappropriate for viewers under 18.

## Editorial qualification

The guarded-field tactic is described as popular legend in the narration and metadata. Do not state it as an uncontested documented event. The video does not claim that potatoes caused the French Revolution; the revolution is only a later chronological transition.
