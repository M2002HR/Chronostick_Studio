# Story architecture — The Shortest War EVER

Status: **Spanish draft for user review**. Independent Stage 01 verification is waived for this episode in `source/research-waiver.json`. The video and transcript are the story premise; this document does not approve the script, an on-screen clock, or a shot.

## Promise, voice and final reveal

- **Opening:** A playful hypothetical about dressing for war and arriving when the cannons are already being put away. It is an original narrator joke, not a purported eyewitness account. It raises the duration question without saying the number.
- **Immediate stakes:** Move from that joke to 08:59 at the palace and five British ships, then rewind to explain how the confrontation formed.
- **Viewer relationship:** Speak in warm, clear, colloquial Spanish using direct address, short turns, a few memorable comparisons and light jokes about maps, diplomatic paperwork, the throne and the deadline. Avoid formal academic hedges and production commentary in the spoken text.
- **Curiosity chain:** Why did Zanzibar matter? Why was a local succession subject to British pressure? Why did Khalid return after 1893? Can the 08:00 ultimatum avert fighting? What changes at 09:00 and 09:05? What remains after Khalid escapes? Only then reveal the final clock.
- **Ending:** Briefly acknowledge the human cost without graphic detail, resolve the political aftermath, return to the imaginary late arrival and reveal 09:38 / 38 minutes in the final paragraph. The surprise answers the hook and the preceding causes explain the duration. Only after that answer, add a short, conversational like/subscribe/next-video invitation with one callback to the missing shoe.

| Viewer invitation | Why stay for the next story? | After the duration and its explanation. | Brief, upbeat call to like, subscribe and keep the next video in mind; a light shoe callback. |
