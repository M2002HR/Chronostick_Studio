## Tag choices

- **Pace:** `[natural pace]` is the main delivery. Four `[rushed pace]` cues cover only news and battle movement; each is followed by `[natural pace]` before explanation resumes. No `[slow pace]` is used because the requested adjustment was small.
- **Emotion:** `[amused]`, `[curiosity]`, `[interest]`, `[enthusiasm]`, `[surprised]` and `[excited]` sustain a warm, engaging story. `[hope]`, `[determination]`, `[thoughtful]` and `[serious]` support the diplomatic tension and human consequences without making deaths a punchline.
- **Style:** `[sarcasm]` marks the authored jokes; `[cautious]` and `[serious]` carry tension and silence; `[shouting]` is confined to the first shot. `[natural]` resets style after pointed turns. `[bold]` and `[authoritative]` serve brief rhetorical or factual pivots.
- **Pauses and sounds:** Six `[short pause]`, three `[medium pause]` and one `[long pause]` mark questions, surrender, casualties, the duration reveal and the closing invitation. One `[giggles]` follows the opening late-arrival joke. Other menu sounds are not needed for this narration.

Scene 06 adds one short pause after the historical conclusion. `[enthusiasm]` carries the like/subscribe/next-video invitation, and `[amused]` lands its brief missing-shoe callback. The invitation comes after the thirty-eight-minute answer.
