## Provisional historical spine

In 1890 Britain announced a protectorate over the Sultanate of Zanzibar, rather than simple British ownership; the [contemporary published notice](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1890/d292) names the Sultan's dominions. Sultan Hamad bin Thuwaini died on 25 August 1896. Khalid bin Barghash seized the throne; the British preferred Hamoud bin Mohammed. After an ultimatum expired at 9:00 a.m. on 27 August, five Royal Navy ships bombarded the palace. Khalid sheltered at the German consulate, and Hamoud was installed afterward, according to the [Historical Association](https://www.history.org.uk/publications/categories/301/resource/7950/the-shortest-war-in-history-the-anglo-zanzibar-wa). This is a provisional factual outline for research, not an approved narration.

## Corrections needed before a Spanish script

- **Duration:** The reference states exactly 38 minutes. The Historical Association lists 38, 40 and 43 minute versions; [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/65353-shortest-war) gives 9:00–9:45. Avoid an exact finish time in script or on-screen text until a chronology is selected and explained.
- **Abolition:** The source's same-day conclusion is wrong. [The UK National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/resources/slavery/source-2a/) dates Zanzibar's abolition to 1897. [Michelle Liebst's research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/law-and-history-review/article/sultans-of-zanzibar-and-the-abolition-of-slavery-in-east-africa/5E0A2E2F535C15592ED9485E6BCC767D) describes earlier Sultanate legislation and local agency as well as British pressure; it does not support an instant freedom montage.
- **Poisoning, quoted speech and comedy:** The source gives Khalid responsibility for Hamad's death, invented Anglo-German conversations, a quoted ultimatum response, a sailor's injury cause, and a Churchill-letter gag. None is approved as a historical statement. The opening sailor scenario is dramatized and should inspire only an original structural idea.
- **Human cost:** The [Historical Association](https://www.history.org.uk/publications/categories/301/resource/7950/the-shortest-war-in-history-the-anglo-zanzibar-wa) reports more than 500 defenders dead and one British marine injured. The transcript calls the 500 “casualties” and adds an unsupported foot injury. Use neutral, qualified language after checking the underlying toll.
- **Khalid's story:** A [scholarly study](https://revistas.ucm.es/index.php/ANQE/en/article/view/ANQE1010110035A) confirms the 1893 and 1896 succession attempts, German consulate refuge, German East Africa transfer and death at Mombasa in 1927. It portrays his later life less casually than the reference's “easy life” joke. His exact detention route and correspondence still need archival checking.
