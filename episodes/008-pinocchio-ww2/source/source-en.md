In 1940 amidst the turmoil of World War II, Walt Disney released one of the most iconic animated films ever, Pinocchio. But how did that release date impact the movie's success? Well, the road to success was anything but smooth. As war raged across the globe, the release of Pinocchio faced numerous challenges. First of all, economic hardships meant that fewer families could afford trips to the movies, impacting box office numbers massively. While the movie cost 2.6 million dollars to produce, it ended up only making 1.4 million in its first run. It also didn't help that the international distribution was severely hampered, limiting the film's global reach. Interestingly, Pinocchio played a role in wartime propaganda efforts together with other Disney characters, to encourage patriotism and support for the war. Surprisingly, this really helped the movie recover financially. And by 1945, the movie had already gathered 84 million dollars in profits. Was this one of the best marketing campaigns ever?
