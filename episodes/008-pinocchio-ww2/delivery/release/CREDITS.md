from reviewed Episode007 assets. New Episode008 placements are reviewed against
actual render frames. These are physical contacts, not a score or ambience bed.

Exact preview-source hashes,100ms/90ms excerpt boundaries, processing, selected
WAV hashes and prior asset-review provenance are in
`audio/sfx/reusable-recordings-r001/manifest.json`. Clip05 retains reviewed native
isolated physical sound at an explicit conservative finishing gain. Other native
music-contaminated soundtracks are fully excluded.

Captions use licensed Montserrat Bold, SIL Open Font License1.1. The exact font
source, installed hash and copied OFL license are preserved in the distribution
export package. The final picture uses immutable MiniMax H3 renders with separate
framewise RealESRGAN upscale provenance; production references and thumbnail
use built-in image generation. Accepted narration was supplied by the user.
