Three of the strangest pets ever kept in history. Number three, Napoleon the War Llama. During Napoleon's campaign in Egypt, the Egyptians wanted to make sure they stayed on his good side. So, they gifted him a llama, which they very creatively called Napoleon. Napoleon accepted and took the llama with him back to France, where he even took him with during key battles during his Italian campaign. The soldiers loved him. Number two, Bobby the Magical Mongoose. Harry Houdini, the masterful magician, had a pet mongoose called Bobby. Houdini claimed could supposedly identify fake money by attacking it. Houdini even went on to incorporate Bobby's abilities into his performances, solidifying Bobby as the Magical Mongoose. Number one, the Presidential Alligator. If you ever wanted to meet the president and don't know what to bring, try an alligator. The sixth president once got a pet alligator gifted. Where did he keep the animal? In the White House's bathroom. Sometimes he forgot to tell his guests though, so they were in for a wild surprise when they had to do their duty.
