## Director and editor decisions

The useful architecture is mask curiosity → compressed religious stakes → plot
and powder → arrest reversal → later symbolic life. Keep that chain close, but
answer why the **modern mask** exists rather than stopping at an unexplained
film quote. The letter moves before arrest; execution is later, not an immediate
cellar event. The king is a plot target, never present in the arrest scene.

Contrast wide/close/prop/reaction coverage and make every cut carry a new question.
The draft's 36 panels are coverage candidates, not a mandatory one-panel-per-word
or final cut quota. With accepted voice, prune redundant mask/barrel inserts and
rebalance the overloaded ending. Very fast editing must remain intelligible.

H3 should create the hard cuts inside each generated 4–7-second editorial clip.
Keep subjects' motion moderate, one action and at most one camera move per shot.
Door opens to a resolved discovery; arrest has two guards and one focal man;
period props remain stable. Cross-file continuity uses resolved states rather
than a physical move requiring a second generation to complete it.

Book, hands, crowded modern scene, undercroft and period exterior need their own
single-image coverage where existing anchors cannot support the planned crop or
state. Portrait/character and full-scene design follows user storyboard review.
Every production image must be entirely stick-world; this neutral rough board
is composition evidence only. No separate manual edit pass is assumed.

camera trajectories, every sub-second cut, source music and audio delivery:
