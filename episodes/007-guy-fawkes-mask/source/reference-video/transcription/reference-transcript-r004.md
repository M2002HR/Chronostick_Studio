This mask, surely you know it. But where is it from and why does it have to do with blowing up the government? Back in the 1500s, England was going through a Christianity crisis. The average person had been mainly Catholic, so team Pope, while the Crown and government started to lean more towards Protestantism. So team no Pope. And one day the authorities were like, you know what, let's make Protestantism the main state religion. In other words, being Catholic was about to be illegal. Wait, what? Said Guy Fawkes, who was a guy. I'm not going to let that happen. So he formed a small gang and together they made a plan to get rid of King James the First and his followers… by blowing up Parliament? Not sure how that would work, but the enthusiasm was there. So on November 5th, 1605, the Guy Gang entered Parliament's basement, placed a whopping 36 gunpowder barrels and… they got arrested. Yeah, not just that, but tortured and their heads were cut off and put on London Bridge. Turns out a Catholic nobleman ratted them out. But beneath this mask there is more than flesh. There's an idea, Mr. Creedy. And ideas are bulletproof.
