# Historical verification

## Verified claims

- Hiroshima was attacked at 8:15 a.m. on August 6, 1945. The City of Hiroshima identifies the aircraft as the B-29 *Enola Gay* and states that the bomb detonated roughly 600 meters above the city: [City of Hiroshima — Atomic bombing](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/048/861/2603_80_e04.pdf).
- The precise death toll is unknown. The City of Hiroshima estimates approximately 140,000 deaths, with a ±10,000 range, by the end of 1945: [City of Hiroshima — The Realities of the Atomic Bombing](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/english/peace/1029920/1009857.html).

## Production interpretation

The elderly couple, breakfast scene, exact minute-long countdown, quoted reassurance, hand clasp, and moment-by-moment actions come from the supplied narration and are treated as a dramatized viewpoint, not as verified identities or eyewitness reconstruction. Visuals must not imply that these specific fictionalized people are documented historical individuals.

The narration wording is preserved in `script/narration-es.md`. The prompts add no further factual claim and avoid graphic victims, spectacle, triumphalism, or invented readable signage.
