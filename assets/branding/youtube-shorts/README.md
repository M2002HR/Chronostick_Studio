# ChronoStick Shorts thumbnail system

The default workflow is prompt-first and generation-optional. Every episode gets a purpose-built thumbnail direction from its own hook, mood, story, and visual assets.

## Channel constants

- approved detailed cinematic ChronoStick world and recurring-character identity
- one truthful visual promise connected to the video's hook or payoff
- a complete one-second hook: immediate subject or stakes plus a specific unresolved question, contradiction, countdown, consequence, or emotional tension
- immediate mobile readability, strong focal hierarchy, clean silhouette separation, and safe margins
- a short curiosity-driving headline rendered inside the generated artwork
- one consistent generated display-lettering system: uppercase very-heavy rounded forms, broad counters, compact spacing, thick near-black outline, shallow 3D depth, and soft drop shadow
- respectful treatment of real people, history, tragedy, and sensitive subjects
- one 9:16 source plus a 2160×3840 PNG delivery master when generation is authorized

## Locked typography system

The accepted Episode 002 thumbnail defines the channel's typography benchmark. Future thumbnails should reproduce its lettering character approximately:

- very heavy, rounded, uppercase display lettering
- warm white setup text and signal yellow-to-gold emphasis when a second level is needed
- thick near-black outline
- shallow bevel or extrusion rather than deep 3D perspective
- soft shadow that separates the words without muddying counters
- generous size and clean spacing for phone-scale reading

This is a visual generation specification, not a separately installed font asset. The final headline must be generated inside the complete thumbnail artwork in the same pass. Do not add, replace, or repair it with an external text overlay. The Episode 002 image may be passed as a typography-only benchmark, but its composition, subjects, wording, and palette distribution are not a reusable template.

## Episode-adaptive choices

Choose the best composition, crop, number of subjects, camera angle, visual metaphor, palette, lighting, headline position, and line breaks for the individual video. The locked rounded display-lettering system remains consistent, but a previous upper/middle/lower layout, cyan background, or subject arrangement is not a mandatory template. Brand coherence comes from the ChronoStick world, the shared type system, high production quality, clarity, and honest curiosity—not from copying one thumbnail.

## Prompt-first workflow

Before any image generation, save the next immutable episode file:

`delivery/youtube/thumbnail-prompt-rNNN.md`

Start from [`thumbnail-prompt.template.md`](thumbnail-prompt.template.md), then replace every placeholder with episode evidence and a production-ready prompt.

It must contain:

1. the video's hook, payoff, mood, and target curiosity gap
2. three distinct thumbnail concepts and a short reason to select one
3. the exact short on-image headline and emphasis plan
4. composition, camera, expression, lighting, color, depth, and mobile-crop direction
5. a reference-role map separating approved episode references from external inspiration
6. the complete generation prompt and negative constraints
7. an acceptance checklist for spelling, extra text, identity, stick-world compliance, truthfulness, hierarchy, and safe margins

Before selection, test each concept at a one-second glance: the viewer should understand what matters and still have one concrete question that only the video can answer. A topic label without tension is not a complete hook. No thumbnail can guarantee clicks; after publication, use real CTR and retention data to judge performance.

Saving this prompt is the default stopping point. Generate only when the user explicitly requests image creation. If prompt writing and generation are requested together, save the prompt first and then generate.

## Reference policy

Approved episode references may be used directly to preserve the ChronoStick style, characters, locations, vehicles, props, and period continuity. Thumbnails from other channels are never channel assets or fixed templates. Inspect them only for abstract lessons such as information density, contrast, hierarchy, and curiosity; do not copy or automatically send them to image generation.

## Generation and review

Generate the complete thumbnail—including the exact headline—as one ChatGPT image. Do not composite or repair text afterward. If the output misspells the headline, adds words, weakens the intended hierarchy, breaks identity/style, or misrepresents the video, reject it and generate a new immutable revision from a revised saved prompt.

Episode 002 remains the typography benchmark and a quality example. Its scene layout and episode-specific art direction are not mandatory visual masters for later topics.
